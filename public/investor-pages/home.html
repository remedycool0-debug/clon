Red Flags of Fraud

Learning how scams work can help you protect your money. Be wary of investment opportunities that you didn’t seek out. Watch out for these red flags:

  1. Promises of high investment returns with little or no risk
  2. Pressure to act now
  3. Fear of missing out or FOMO
  4. Fake testimonials
  5. Promises of great wealth
  6. Suspicious payment methods
